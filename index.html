September Tips 九月旅遊貼士

📅
Travel Late September 九月下旬

More comfortable weather. Cooler evenings & clearer skies for outdoor walks. 天氣更涼爽，適合戶外活動。

👕
Pack Smart 聰明打包

Breathable clothing + light rain jacket + folding umbrella. 透氣衣物、輕薄雨衣、摺疊傘必備。

🚕
DiDi 滴滴 for Seniors

Use 滴滴出行 DiDi frequently. Avoid long walks in heat for seniors. 長者應多乘滴滴，避免長途步行。

🏛️
Museums at 2pm 午後進館

Schedule indoor museums & aquarium during 1–4pm heat peak. 午後最熱時安排室內景點。

🎡
Book Early 提早預訂

Book 迪士尼 Premier Access and 上海中心 tower tickets online well in advance. 景點門票需提前預訂。

🌀
Carry Fans 隨身攜帶扇子

Portable handheld fans + umbrella make a real difference outdoors. 手持電風扇是九月戶外必備。
